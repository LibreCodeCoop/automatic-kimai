# Community conduct

Our goal is a constructive, accessible free-software community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to use respectful language, discuss technical disagreements on their merits, protect other contributors' privacy and refrain from harassment, intimidation, discriminatory attacks or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may moderate issues and pull requests, request edits, or restrict participation when conduct prevents productive collaboration. Security concerns should be disclosed privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before accepting public contributions, the project's maintainers must designate a private way to report serious conduct concerns. Do not post private complaints or sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.
